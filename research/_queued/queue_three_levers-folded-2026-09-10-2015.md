## GENERATION MEETING INPUT — three levers, queued by Jason 2026-09-10

Not three parallel research projects. TWO CHANGES TO THE GENERATOR and
ONE CANDIDATE. Only the third spends research budget; the first two
change what comes out of the funnel and consume no trials.

Discovery LEADS the meeting that takes these up (AMENDMENT v2.2 order).
These items are its INPUT, not a decision already made — the meeting
rules on scope, on sequencing, and on whether all three proceed at once
or staged. The Integrity Gate's per-variable resurrection rulings apply
in full.

THE DIAGNOSIS BEHIND ALL THREE (Jason + Claude, 2026-09-10): this
project has built an excellent FILTER and a thin FUNNEL. Nearly all
machinery to date exists to kill ideas properly, and it works — 97 idea
families, 239 trials, one candidate through the full bar. But ideas come
from exactly one place: LEARN's memory of what this project has already
tried. There is no external input of any kind. LEARN's unexplored list is
shrinking not because the market ran out of edges but because the project
ran out of its own ideas. Every scan so far has asked one question in
different clothes: does an instrument's own state predict its own forward
return.

### G1. MECHANISM-FIRST GENERATION — do this first

Invert the current order. Today a candidate is found by scanning and the
mechanism is written afterwards; the project has ZERO pre-registered
mechanisms in its history, and every mechanism document on disk is marked
POST-HOC.

Instead: start from a structural reason someone is FORCED to trade badly
at a specific time — index rebalances, options-expiry hedging flows,
month-end rebalancing, futures roll, scheduled-event positioning — write
the mechanism claim and its falsifiable prediction FIRST, freeze it, and
only then measure.

Costs no trials. Changes the prior odds at the source. A candidate with a
prediction made before measurement is worth far more than one explained
afterwards, and this is the only way the project ever gets one.

### G2. AN EXTERNAL KNOWLEDGE INPUT — do this second

The largest structural gap. The project has never looked outside itself
for a hypothesis. Published work on futures market microstructure and
anomalies is a real supply of ideas that ARRIVE WITH MECHANISM CLAIMS
ALREADY ATTACHED.

Much of it is dead, overfit, or arbitraged away. That is what the
Integrity Gate is for, and every imported idea takes a resurrection
ruling and the full entry bar like any other. But as GENERATION INPUT it
is enormously better than re-examining the same five descriptors.

Meeting to decide: what the source actually is, who screens it, and how
an imported claim is distinguished in the ledger from one this project
originated. Costs no trials.

### G3. CROSS-INSTRUMENT DIVERGENCE — do this third, and deliberately

NOT "does ZN's level predict NQ" — that framing is closed three separate
ways (hyp-000031, hyp-000034, hyp-000035) and is NOT to be resurrected.
The untried framing is NQ and ZN, or NQ and VXN, COMING APART:
relative-value / co-movement breakdown as a state variable. Different
mechanism claim, and the data is already on disk (NQ, ZN, VXN — note ES
is NOT on disk, contrary to an earlier NEXT_UP claim).

This is the one item that SPENDS RESEARCH BUDGET. It raises the trial
count and tightens the Sidak correction on everything still in flight.
Statistical states that cost at the meeting before it is scoped.

### WHY THIS ORDER — the argument, not a preference

Run G1 and G2 first and G3 becomes THE FIRST CANDIDATE IN THIS PROJECT'S
HISTORY GENERATED UNDER THE NEW METHOD, carrying a mechanism written
before its numbers exist.

Run G3 first and it produces one more post-hoc mechanism document — and
the opportunity is spent on the single candidate best suited to prove
whether the new approach actually works.

Same three items. Different order. Materially different outcome.

### STANDING WARNING, from the Integrity Gate
