# Sourcing entry template (front end, September 12th, 2026)

Every candidate enters through this template, is ruled on by Mechanism and
LEARN, and only then becomes a map row + Idea Inventory entry + mechanism
doc. An entry with a blank in any required field is not an entry.

- Working name:
- Source (paper with citation and year / practitioner claim / Observatory
  relationship):
- Claim (one sentence, falsifiable):
- Instrument(s): free field. NQ is the default execution vehicle, not the
  search boundary. State which contract should carry the SIGNAL and which
  the TRADE, and why.
- Holding period: must resolve in hours to a few days (hard filter,
  applied here at sourcing AND again at monetization).
- Forced or constrained participant: who, and what forces them.
- Mechanism: why the participant's constraint leaves a footprint.
- INSTRUMENT-CHOICE GATE (all five answered):
  1. Which contract should lead under this mechanism?
  2. Why would the effect exist at MINUTES rather than milliseconds?
  3. Is NQ actually the cheapest / most liquid way to express it?
  4. Directional NQ, or relative NQ vs another index?
  5. Does it survive a beta-neutral benchmark (NULL 2) if cross-index, or
     the unconditional-drift benchmark (NULL 1) if directional?
- Footprint in 1-minute bars (or bars built from them) and the natural
  resolution, with the reason the mechanism operates at that scale.
- Exact published / pre-registered specification (windows, thresholds,
  buckets) -- the one shot is at THIS spec.
- Publication date (for literature entries) and post-publication decay
  evidence. A null on post-publication data may mean "decayed," record it
  that way.
- Published effect size and sample.
- Resurrection ruling: checked against the ledger by NAME and by WHAT IS
  MEASURED; list the adjacent closed families and say why this is
  different. Integrity signs.
- Known confounds, and the pre-registered check that separates them.
- Product track: directional alpha / relative-value alpha / risk-execution
  alpha.
- Natural realization path (time-based, trailing, directional push, etc.).
- Data needed and whether it is on disk.
- KILL RULE: if the response is concentrated in the first 1-minute bar,
  the family terminates -- no re-tuning toward slower variants.
- Multiplicity: number of cells to be registered in SCAN_REGISTRY.

RANKING RULE (binding, Jason, September 12th): entries are ordered by
EXPECTED INFORMATION GAIN x EDGE POTENTIAL -- what the result teaches us
either way, times what it is worth if real -- NOT by the probability of a
positive Discovery result. A low-odds entry that decides a data purchase or
a design principle outranks a likely-but-minor pass. State both factors in
one line each at the bottom of every entry.
