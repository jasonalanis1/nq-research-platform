## WHAT NEW INFORMATION THIS RUN PRODUCED

**A placebo failure and a separability pass are not contradictory, and the
project now has a worked example of the difference.** The mechanical suite said
yesterday's value does 74% of the work; the Director test said 87% survives when
yesterday's value is held fixed. Both numbers are correct. A lagged predictor can
be individually informative *and* leave most of the current predictor's
information intact, because the two share a persistent driver without being
substitutes. Read alone, the placebo number would have killed a genuinely new
candidate. Read alone, the separability number would have missed that the
underlying variable persists strongly enough to matter for sizing.

**The consequence is a method note worth keeping:** a high placebo score is a
reason to run the residualised test, not a verdict on its own. The suite should
keep flagging it — the flag did its job by forcing this analysis — but the
disposition belongs to the stage that can hold the suspect fixed.
