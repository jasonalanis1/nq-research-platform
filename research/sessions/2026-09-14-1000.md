## What broke

**Three false verdicts, all caught by the suite's own first runs, all fixed and
locked in with regression tests.** Worth listing individually because the
pattern is the same each time and it is the main lesson of this cycle:

1. **roll_date_contamination red on normal calendar exposure.** The first
   implementation flagged the full 8-calendar-day window up to quarterly
   expiry — about 32 sessions a year, a **12.7% calendar base rate**. Any
   candidate that trades most days lands ~12–13% of its observations in that
   window and trips the spec's 5% threshold *every time*. Stack A duly came
   back RED at 9.4% — which is **below** the base rate. Narrowed to the actual
   roll days (expiry Friday and the day before, ~3.0% base rate), which is what
   makes the frozen 5% threshold mean "clustered on roll days" instead of
   "exists". The check now also reports the calendar base rate next to the
   observed share so the number is readable. Stack A: RED → GREEN at 1.7%.
2. **cost_sensitivity greening an effect that had inverted.** The spec's literal
   rule is "red if the CI crosses zero under 2× costs". Taken alone, that greens
   a candidate whose edge has moved *cleanly to the wrong side* of the null — its
   CI does not cross zero, it sits credibly below it. A +0.02R edge on a 20-point
   stop is smaller than the 2× round-trip cost (0.075R) and lands at −0.055R,
   credibly negative, and the check called it a pass. Now red on a sign flip as
   well as on a CI that crosses.
3. **placebo red on hyp-000142 — a Holdout-passed fact.** The shifted-signal
   (t+1) placebo reached 0.228 against the real 0.284. Before reporting that a
   promoted fact fails its placebo, I measured the thing that would explain it:
   **P(VXN HIGH tomorrow | HIGH today) = 0.79**, and the t+1 selection
   **re-selects 78.8% of the real sample**. A shifted placebo is only a placebo
   if it selects different days; for a *persistent state* (a volatility tercile,
   a regime flag) it reproduces the effect by construction and would red every
   persistent-state candidate in the project. The shifted leg is now reported but
   does not gate when its overlap with the real selection exceeds 50% — measured
   and disclosed per candidate, never assumed. The **inverted** leg, which is
   what actually asks whether the direction means anything, still gates and hyp-142
   passes it clearly (−0.086 against +0.284, opposite sign, a third the size).

On (3) specifically, since it is the kind of change the project's anti-tuning
rules exist to prevent: this is not a threshold loosened to let a particular
candidate through. It is a structural correction for an entire class of
candidate, justified by a measured property (the overlap) that is computed and
reported for every candidate, and the leg that does the real work still gates.
Both regression tests assert the guard *does not* fire on a non-persistent
signal, so it cannot become a blanket excuse.

## WHAT NEW INFORMATION THIS RUN PRODUCED

**hyp-000142 passes the mechanical suite cleanly** (5 green, 0 red) — the first
independent structural audit of a promoted fact in this project, and it holds up:

**Stack A carries two structural defects that were never the reason it was
closed.** It died as a powered null on its registered outcome. The suite says
that, additionally, its effect is era-dependent (first half credible, second not,
signs disagreeing) and radically concentrated (top 5% of observations carry 188%
of a small net effect). This matters for the *format*, not the candidate: the
conditional-stack format's one completed trial had structural problems a
mechanical pre-screen would have surfaced before the trial was spent. With one
powered null left before the format closes, and U28's look table finding nothing
that stands out, the honest reading is that the format has not yet been given a
candidate worth its remaining attempt.

**A methodological pattern, now three for three.** Every structural check built
in the last two cycles was wrong on its first contact with real data, in the same
direction each time — it fired on something that was normal. The interaction
table ranked main effects as interactions; the roll check flagged ordinary
calendar exposure; the shifted placebo flagged signal persistence. The common
cause is that each check compared a candidate against a baseline that did not
account for the *structure the data already has*. The generalisable rule: a
structural check needs its own base rate before its threshold means anything,
and the base rate has to be computed, not assumed. All three now compute and
report it.
