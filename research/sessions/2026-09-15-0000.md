| hour (ET) | effect | 90% CI |
|---|---:|---|
| 10:00 | **+0.0835** | (+0.068, +0.100) |
| 08:00 | +0.0745 | (+0.056, +0.092) |

**London ranks 4th of 23.** London minus the best other hour: **−0.0333, CI
(−0.057, −0.008)** — entirely below zero, so it is credibly *smaller*, not merely
indistinguishable. Six of 23 hours clear the same bar. The claim is true and it is
one member of a family: the baseline is the mean RTH hour, dragged down by the
quiet afternoon, so any above-average hour clears it.
