## Follow-up 3 — the standing preflight rule, as a gate

`src/cycle_preflight.py:reference_data_gate()`. For **every** series in
`research/ledger/data_coverage.json` it compares coverage end against the session date and **blocks the
cycle steps that depend on that series**, by name, naming the series as the reason
(`STEP_DEPENDENCIES`). Written into the receipt (`research/_cycle_preflight.json`,
`steps.reference_data_gate`) and reported by `src/session_report.py:reference_data_gate_lines()` in
section 4 OPERATIONS.

Blocked today: `step3_paper_scoring`, `step3_b2_risk_state_decision`, `step4_salvage_check`,
`salvage_reruns` (all four series), `step4_specify_S009a` (FOMC/CPI/NFP), `step4_specify_S010a` (VXN).

**Blocked ≠ aborted.** `INDEPENDENT_STEPS` still run: sourcing, specifying, freezing and screening
price-only candidates on the Discovery slice, and close-out. A stale series is never an excuse to idle
a cycle (s.3 Step 2).

**Fail closed on a missing entry.** A series named as a dependency with no coverage entry at all, or
with no `last_date`, is treated as **stale**. "We have no record of how far it reaches" is not evidence
that it reaches far enough — the exact shape of the original bug. Unreadable coverage blocks *every*
dependent step.

Recorded as standing and attributed to Jason (September 16th) in `research/NEXT_UP.md` and
`research/CYCLE_PROMPT.md` (Step 1). Tests: 10 new in `tests/test_cycle_preflight.py` — a stale series
blocks its dependents and is reported; a current series blocks nothing; coverage exactly at the session
date is current; a missing entry and a null `last_date` are both stale; the independent steps still run;
unreadable coverage blocks everything; the reruns-owed line appears and flips when coverage is current.
