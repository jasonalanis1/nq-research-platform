| opening range (10:00 ET) | mean midday range | multiplier |
|---|---:|---:|
| LOW | 40.1 pts | x0.76 |

That is 14.3 points of expected range, ≈7.2 points of B2 stop distance. The
screening rule is cleared (0.75pt cost is 2.8–3.7% of the stop) but the honest
number is the increment: **the cost is 10.5% of the change this fact makes.**

never by product.

**A caveat this stage found that the earlier ones did not.** The ratio effect is
regime-stable (+0.49 / +0.29 / +0.46 across prior-day-range terciles) but the
**points** effect flips sign in the MID stratum (−3.5 pts). Both are correct: the
multiplier applies to each session's own trailing average, so the ratio is the
decision unit. But "the effect scales with volatility" must not be read as "more
points in every regime", and the Director's carried-forward observation is
narrowed accordingly.
