**Mechanism** (spec s.2): in the first thirty minutes the market auctions off the
overnight imbalance, and the other side of that flow is taken by short-term liquidity
providers whose business is assuming the opening move is an overreaction. They build
that inventory *inside* the opening range; the opposite extreme is where their
assumption is wrong. On a decisive close beyond an extreme their position is underwater
and the covering is mechanical, deadline-driven and in the direction of the break —
that is who is on the other side. The project's own validated magnitude fact sets the
scale: a wide first half-hour predicts a midday range about half a trailing average
wider with **symmetric** excursions (hyp-000162 / M30, research/KNOWLEDGE.md:54,
Director ADVANCE at research/KNOWLEDGE.md:56). Hence a stop **scaled to the opening
range** (0.50 × width) instead of B3's unconditioned far-side stop, and a **1.5R**
target because the adverse excursion is enlarged at least as much as the favourable one.

**Not B3.** base_entry_b3.py (src/base_entry_b3.py:11) is frozen plumbing with an
explicitly unconditioned stop and target and no claimed edge; S007 shares only the
event, and adds a 0.10 × width decisiveness requirement, a 10:00–13:00 ET window
(B3 stops at 11:30), the scaled stop, the justified target and a named failure list.
