## What broke
Two self-caught bugs while writing `tests/test_order_path.py`, both fixed before
the suite was called green:
1. The passing-state test fixture used `remaining_budget_usd=0.0`;
   `capital_protection.daily_loss_cap_hit()` fail-closes (returns True) whenever
   remaining budget is `<= 0`, by design — every "should pass" test was silently
   blocked until the fixture used a positive remaining budget (300.0).
2. A test tried to raise `CapitalConfig.max_contracts` for one case via
   `dataclasses.replace()` on the module-level `CONFIG` and reassigning the
   module attribute; `capital_protection.pre_order_check`'s `cfg` parameter binds
   its default at *function-definition* time to the original `CapitalConfig`
   instance, so replacing the module attribute never reaches it. Fixed by
   mutating the frozen instance's field in place (`object.__setattr__`, restored
   in `finally`) instead of replacing the object.
