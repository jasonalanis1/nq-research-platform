**The mechanism** (spec s.1, research/infrastructure/strategy-specs/S009-afternoon-vwap-completion-continuation.md:15):
institutional parent orders worked to a full-day VWAP / percentage-of-volume benchmark have a **fixed
quantity**, a **clock deadline** (the cash close) and a **back-loaded volume curve**. Quantity stranded
on the wrong side of the session VWAP by the early afternoon has to be worked into the remaining hours
*regardless of price* — the algo is measured against VWAP and cannot wait for a better one. That is
one-directional price-insensitive flow, and it predicts **continuation** of the benchmark gap. The
trigger quantity is the distance of price from the session's own VWAP, not the day's directional move,
which is what makes its trade selection different from S008's even though both end up long the
afternoon's direction.

IB-breakout revamp entries hyp-000065/028/135 are bottom tier, "stays dead"); not H118/hyp-000121-134
(a 10-day drift from an ATR-normalised VWAP distance, reversion direction); not
`src/detect_vwap_reversion.py`'s 2-sigma fade (opposite direction, opposite side); not M16 (first-30 →
last-30 momentum, clean null).

direction **sign(D)**; entry the next bar's open; stop **0.50 × RNG**; target **1.5R**; flat **15:55 ET**;
