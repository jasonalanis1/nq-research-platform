   six windows x four states x three terciles; the largest |z| is 2.2 — about what
   chance produces in 72 draws. No direction footprint anywhere. This is the
   time-of-day x state cut review D asked for, and it agrees with the daily/10-day
   scans: nothing directional to chase in these four states.
2. **Range persistence is everywhere (known).** Every strong footprint is a range
   cell, and every one restates the validated volatility facts inside each window:
   compressed prior day -> smaller first-30/morning/midday range; coil -> smaller;
   elevated VXN -> larger in every window including the last hour. Useful for the
   Risk/State Engine (U13) as *within-session* range shape; not new facts.
   Self-referential cell excluded: overnight window range x overnight_range_vs_atr
   state is the same quantity (z=+17.9 / -15.8 are tautologies, not footprints).
3. **M29 Stage 0 (implied-minus-realized gap): DISTINCT from VXN level (47% tercile
   overlap, corr +0.33) — may proceed to a registered family. But the SIGN is
   opposite to the mechanism doc's pre-registered P1:** HIGH gap days have LARGER
   range in every window (first30 z=+7.4, morning +7.4, last hour +6.8), LOW gap days
   smaller. The doc predicted dampening (HIGH gap -> lower range). Two honest
   readings: (a) the gap is partly a VXN-level echo (corr +0.33) and the residual
   test (P2) is what matters; (b) a wide implied-over-realized gap may mark
   anticipated-event / regime-shift days rather than dealer long-gamma. Per the
   one-shot rule, Stage 1 tests P1 AS WRITTEN; if it fails, that is attempt 1 of 2,
   and any reversed-sign attempt needs its own mechanism, written before the scan.
   Recorded in the M29 doc, Section 10.
4. **What this cost:** no hypothesis ID, no scan registered, ~2 minutes of compute.
