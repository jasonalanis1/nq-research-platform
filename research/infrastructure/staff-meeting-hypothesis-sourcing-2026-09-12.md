Convened by the Research Director at Jason's request, September 12th, 8:31 am CT.
Question: the testing pipeline works and the idea supply ran dry. How do we find the next hypotheses?
Context Jason set: keep the 2-hour operations cadence regardless; this is a knowledge gap to close, not a reason to idle; he is also polling outside AIs with a shared brief (research/infrastructure/... the prompt in chat) before deciding.

## Positions (every agent recorded)

LEARN — Source quality is the lever, and the ledger proves it: pattern-guess 0%, intuition ~1 in 8, behavior-first ~1 in 3 for finding something real. Literature-sourced entries are the highest-prior pool we have never used, BUT every one must clear a resurrection check against ~40 closed families before it enters. Two of the three named papers sit next to closed ground: intraday momentum (Gao et al.) is adjacent to hyp-000076 (opening volume imbalance) and the IB-breakout family — different claim (first-half-hour SIGN -> last-half-hour SIGN), not a resurrection, but the ruling must be written. Overnight-vs-intraday (Lou/Polk/Skouras) is adjacent to the gap-fade family and H116 — again distinct (two return streams as separate clienteles, not a fade), ruling required.

DISCOVERY — Two cheap actions open genuinely new ground: (1) fetch ES 1-minute bars (L0 schema, fits the existing budget) and open NQ-vs-ES relative value as an axis with its own forced participant (sector-rotation and index-arb flow); (2) stand up a standing SOURCING producer — an agent that reads papers and practitioner material and emits map-ready entries in a fixed template (claim, participant, mechanism, footprint in 1-min bars, published effect size, post-publication decay, resurrection check). That is the shelf refill rate the engine never had.

MECHANISM — Pushback on "published = pre-vetted mechanism." Most anomaly catalogs are statistical regularities with a story attached after the fact; mining them is pattern-guessing with citations. Admit only effects with a named forced or constrained participant. The three named ones qualify: intraday momentum (late-informed and hedging flow), overnight/intraday tug-of-war (retail-overnight vs institutional-intraday clienteles), hedging-demand intraday momentum (option dealers' gamma). The practitioner stream qualifies only once a claim is translated into a mechanical proxy; "trend day vs range day" is admissible as a research PROGRAM, not as one hypothesis.

STATISTICAL — First-order item, before any new directional draw: the null is wrong. Long-only drift claims have been tested against zero on an asset that drifted +0.18-0.20 ATR per 3 days over Discovery. Fix project-wide: every directional claim is expressed relative to the unconditional drift (or as a sign-adjusted / long-minus-short construction that is baseline-free). Run the H118 all-days-baseline diagnostic now — it is cheap and touches nothing frozen. Note that INTRADAY effects (open-to-close windows) are much less exposed, because most of NQ's drift is earned overnight — which is itself the Lou/Polk/Skouras finding and one more reason to test it. Power is not a concern for daily-n intraday effects (~1,600 usable days).

BLIND INTEGRITY GATE — Two cautions. (1) Post-publication decay is real and large (roughly half the effect on average in the literature on this); our Discovery slice is post-publication for most candidates and Validation more so, so a null may mean "decayed," not "never existed" — record the publication date on every literature entry and read nulls accordingly. (2) Having READ the paper means the test is no longer naive: pre-register the exact published specification, run it once, and do not re-tune windows to "find" the effect. That is the same one-shot rule, stated for a new temptation.

MONETIZATION — The three survivors are all volatility facts, and the money in this project may be in sizing and exits, not entries. Supports the day-type program precisely because it is where existing validated knowledge compounds instead of starting from zero. Insists the "natural realization path" question is asked at scoping for every new entry, not after Validation.

PORTFOLIO — The day-type classifier is the three validated range facts combined. Before it becomes a program, show they are not one latent state: the midday-lull fact is already 82.5% separable from the overnight coil; the range-contraction fact has not been checked against either. Owns that check.

RESEARCH INTEGRITY — The structural conclusion stands: the participants who leave money on the table are not visible in 1-minute OHLCV, and scheduled public flows are the most competed in the world. Sourcing from the literature does not change that ceiling; it changes the quality of what we test inside it. On the practitioner stream: a claim that cannot be falsified as stated is not an entry — translate it or drop it. On the data question: the hedging-demand paper is the honest reason to buy options open-interest data — a published mechanism to test, not a fishing trip — and should be the first thing the purchase is spent on if Jason lifts the ceiling.

OPERATIONS MANAGER — Cadence stays at 2 hours per Jason. Cycles get a new owed item: SOURCING. "TOP-UP OWED" no longer means "invent a scope"; it means "run a sourcing pass and produce map-ready entries for the Director." Empty map is a resting state, not an alert; the acknowledgment mechanism already reflects that.
