# Momentum-Continuation Prospective Test -- Frozen Spec (exp-069)

## Status: FROZEN 2026-09-08, before any Validation-slice number is looked at.

## Why this is a Validation-slice test, not a fresh Discovery test

exp-066 and exp-068 (batch-2026-09-08-bar-behavior-1, both REJECTED as
pre-registered) each independently showed a statistically credible
effect in the OPPOSITE direction from their predicted reversal: NQ
tended to CONTINUE in the original direction after a rejection wick at
resistance, and after a large-range day's follow-through got
contradicted. That opposite-direction finding was only visible AFTER
looking at Discovery-slice results -- so any hypothesis that predicts
"continuation" using those same conditions on that same Discovery data
would be guaranteed to look good (it's the same numbers, sign-flipped),
which is not a real test of anything. The Path-to-Profitability
Advisor's first suggestion (treat it as an ordinary fresh Discovery
hypothesis) was reviewed and overridden here for exactly this reason --
mirroring the sign of an already-observed result on the same data is
not independent evidence, however the hypothesis gets labeled.

The honest test: treat this exactly like exp-061 (ZN bond-lead)  was
treated when it looked promising on Discovery -- require ONE
pre-registered prospective test on data this specific direction has
never touched. That's the Validation slice here.

## Frozen hypothesis

For each of the 2 conditions below (reusing the exact mechanical
definitions from exp-066 and exp-068, unmodified), on the VALIDATION
slice only:

**(a) Rejection wick at resistance -> CONTINUATION.** Day T's upper
wick >= 2.0x body AND Day T's High >= prior day's High. Predicted:
NEXT day's return is POSITIVE (continuation, mirroring exp-066's
disclosed Discovery finding).

**(b) Failed-breakout day -> ORIGINAL-DIRECTION continuation.** Day T
large-range (>=1.5x trailing 20-day avg range) with Day T+1 in the
opposite direction. Predicted: Day T+2's return is signed toward Day
T's ORIGINAL direction (not T+1's, mirroring exp-068's disclosed
Discovery finding -- the exact opposite prediction from exp-068's
original frozen spec).

Statistically credible = 90% bootstrap CI on the signed-to-prediction
mean entirely above zero, same gate as every other Step 1 test in this
project.

## What passing means here

Because this already skipped the normal "PROMISING on Discovery, then
confirm on Validation" two-step (Discovery evidence here came from an
unplanned observation, not a planned Step 1 pass), a pass on Validation
is treated as equivalent evidentiary weight to a normal Discovery
PROMISING result, NOT as a fully-confirmed prospective validation --
it still needs its own real confirmation before any live authorization,
same as ZN would have needed a second look if it had passed. A pass
here means "worth designing a real costed rule and testing that," not
"done." A fail closes both conditions for good -- no further retuning,
no trying a third variant.
