## The budget

$2.50/side commission + 1 tick/side slippage = **$6.00 per micro round trip**
(ASSUMED, `src/paper_book.py`). MNQ pays **$2.00 per index point**, so the round trip
is **3.00 index points**. The budget written into the spec *before* any outcome was
looked at: a candidate must show a **gross expectancy of at least 9.0 points per
trade** — 3× the cost — so that costs are about a third of the edge rather than
S007's seventeen times it.

## What the candidate was

Late-day continuation driven by constant-leverage rebalancing: a 3× fund that ends an
up day with too little exposure must **buy** into the 16:00 ET NAV clock, an inverse
fund must buy on the same day, both must sell on a down day, and the notional is
roughly proportional to the day's return. Price-insensitive, must-complete flow,
largest on the largest days. Entry 15:00 ET on `|M| ≥ 0.5 × trailing-20 median
09:30–15:00 range`, direction `sign(M)`, stop `0.50 × that range`, target 1.5R, flat
15:55 ET, 1 micro.

**The frequency half of the design worked.** 0.4390 trades/session → **55.3 trades in
126 sessions, NOT SLOW.**

Median stop on the specified cell is **46.4 points** — the stop-and-target structure
is genuinely measured in tens of points, as the budget demands. The edge is not. Both
halves are required and only one is present.

## Four things this establishes

1. **THE BUDGET CHECK IS CHEAP AND IT WORKS.** S007 cost a freeze, a screen, a
   salvage and a kill to learn that +$0.35 a trade does not pay $6.00. S008 cost one
   descriptive script and was refused at SPECIFY under the directive's own
   Monetization ruling ("no credible path" → LEARN without a screen, s.4). That is
   the S007 lesson operating as a gate rather than as a post-mortem.
2. **THE INTRADAY DIRECTIONAL DRIFT AVAILABLE TO THIS PROJECT ON NQ IS 1–6 POINTS.**
   Thirty-six continuation cells spanning six decision times and four move thresholds
   all land in that band, and the reversion family's gross edge is +0.04R with a flat
   profile across distance buckets. This is now the reference range for any intraday
   candidate: it must explain why it is outside a band that nothing tested so far has
   left.
3. **THE VALIDATED MAGNITUDE FACTS SIZE A TRADE; THEY DO NOT CREATE ONE.** The
   quiet-midday fact selects days whose afternoon range is *smaller in absolute
   points* (median 27.4 vs 38.2) — expansion relative to a compressed midday is not
   expansion in the units a fixed cost is paid in, and using it would have made the
   cost problem worse, not better. The wide-opening-range fact splits 48.3% of
   sessions and leaves the morning continuation at +3.1 / −0.8 / +0.7 / +1.1 points
   (t = 1.05 / −0.30 / 0.27 / 0.43). A wider expected range widens the stop it is
   asked to size; it does not supply the edge that stop is there to protect. This is
   the same answer Stack A (hyp-000161) gave about the expected-range regime as an
   entry filter, arriving from the cost side.
4. **AMENDMENT 1'S FREQUENCY PREFERENCE AND THE COST BUDGET PULL AGAINST EACH OTHER
   ON THIS INSTRUMENT, AND THE BUDGET IS THE BINDING ONE.** The two candidates that
   clear the 40-trades-in-six-months bar (S007 at 91.5, S008 at 55.3) are exactly the
   two whose per-trade edge cannot pay a $6.00 round trip; the only strategy in the
   book with a real per-trade edge, S003 at +$379 and +0.396R, holds for a month and
   projects 1.3 trades in six months. Stated as an observation for LEARN, not as a
   proposal: nothing here is a rule change, Amendment 1 stands exactly as Jason wrote
   it, and the SLOW label already handles the strategies the clock cannot fit. What
   the cycle does with it is order its own sourcing: **ask the budget question first,
   and let the fire rate be whatever the surviving structure's fire rate is.**

## Where a per-trade edge could still come from

Not from a filter on any of the above — no knowable condition in the S007 salvage
menu found one, and this study found the family-level reason why. It has to come from
a larger structure: a **measured** cost basis from B4b (the assumption is 3.00 points
and the measurement could be materially smaller), a longer hold in which the forced
flow accumulates, or a mechanism whose forced participant moves tens of points rather
than ones. The S008 mechanism is not refuted. What is refuted is that it is worth
$6.00 a trade.
