## Discovery — proposed state variables

**D1. `nq_zn_divergence`** -- NQ's realized daily return relative to
what its trailing co-movement with ZN (10-Year Treasury futures) would
predict. Mechanism claim: NQ and ZN both price the same macro
information (growth/rates expectations) through different lenses;
when NQ's move sharply outruns or lags what ZN's move implies, that gap
reflects either NQ-specific flow not yet shared by the rates market, or
a temporary dislocation -- both plausible, and expected to partially
correct in the following session(s) as cross-asset arbitrage capital
narrows the gap (a testable, FALSIFIABLE reversion claim, not a
one-directional story). Horizon: short (intraday/overnight/1-2 day),
per Standing Research Priority.

**D2. `nq_vxn_divergence`** -- NQ's realized daily return relative to
what its trailing co-movement with VXN (NASDAQ-100 implied vol) would
predict, in the opposite sense from D1's typical relationship (NQ up,
VXN normally down). Mechanism claim: when NQ rallies (or sells off)
without the VOL market confirming by how much it usually does, that is
options-market skepticism about the move's durability -- a
"non-confirmation" signal, expected to predict reversion toward the
implied-vol-consistent path. Distinct mechanism from D1 (rates-market
co-movement vs. options-market confirmation), distinct counterparty
(cross-asset value arbitrage vs. options-market-informed skepticism).
Horizon: short, same as D1.

**Implementation, both variables (fixed before any result is seen):**
  beta_20 = rolling 20-day OLS beta of NQ daily return on the other
    instrument's daily return, window ending at day i-2 (i.e. the beta
    itself uses no data from day i-1 or later -- doubly lagged to avoid
    any lookahead into the residual it will be applied to).
  predicted_ret[i-1] = beta_20 * other_instrument_return[i-1]
  residual[i-1] = nq_return[i-1] - predicted_ret[i-1]
  state[i] = residual[i-1] / trailing_20d_std_of_residual (z-score),
    knowable as of day i's RTH open -- same no-lookahead anchor
    convention as every existing descriptor.
  Bucketed into terciles (low/mid/high), consistent with every prior
  scan. LOW = NQ underperformed the instrument-implied path (large
  negative residual); HIGH = NQ overperformed it.
  Pre-registered reversion prediction: LOW bucket -> positive forward
  drift, HIGH bucket -> negative forward drift (opposite-signed,
  monotonic across low/mid/high) -- NOT "low is good," a directional
  claim tied to the mechanism, checkable and falsifiable at Discovery
  time itself (this is a genuine a-priori prediction about SHAPE, made
  before the scan runs, which ordinary Discovery-stage exploration does
  not usually get to make).
  Horizons: [1, 2, 3, 5] trading days -- short end only, per Standing
  Research Priority; the 10-day tail is deliberately excluded this scan
  (if a candidate survives at 5d and the shape argues for extending, that
  is a SEPARATE, differently-motivated attempt later, not folded in here).
