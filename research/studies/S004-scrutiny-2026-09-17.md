## Why S004 got this and the others did not

S004 is H118's lineage — the family that passed Discovery, Validation **and** Holdout
and turned out to be the stock market going up
(`research/studies/h118-baseline-diagnostic-2026-09-12.md`). Its screen came back
enormous: 554 trades, +76.0131 gross pt/trade, +$82,782.06 on the MNQ-market decision
basis, all four cost combinations positive. Its own-drift baseline arm — the identical
trade on every session with the descriptor, filter off — earns **+60.7698 pt/trade**.
About **80% of the headline is the index going up**. The genuine margin is
**+15.2433 pt/trade**, and the screening agent itself wrote the caveat into the ledger
row: that is +0.0118 R, the same order as the +0.1162 R whose block bootstrap failed to
clear zero when H118 was killed, on 10-session holds that overlap heavily.

The screen's gate is a point estimate. This file measures the four things that decide
whether that point estimate means anything. **Intervals are used here as MEASUREMENT,
not as a promotion gate** (directive s.12 retired the CI bar as a gate and this file does
not reinstate it).

## The population

`src/screen_s004_own_drift_baseline.py` produced two arms over the same 2,101-session
Discovery slice. Arm A (554 trades) is a strict subset of arm B (1,663 trades) and every
shared trade agrees to 1e-6 — verified in `load_table()`. So the two arms are not two
strategies; they are **one trade and one selection rule**. The margin is entirely a
question of *which sessions were picked*, which is what makes the tests below the right
ones. 2015-02-02 … 2021-09-16.

## 1. Overlap-corrected significance — the margin does not clear zero

House convention: `src/gate_conditions_hyp162.py` resamples blocks of **consecutive
sessions** and re-derives the split inside each resample;
`src/baseline_relative.py:block_bootstrap_diff_ci` uses block = the overlap length.
Both are applied, at block 10 (the hold) and block 20 (twice the hold). 3,000 resamples,
seed 7, 90% intervals.

| unit | block | margin | CI90 | clears zero | P(margin ≤ 0) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| points | 10 | **+15.2432** | −0.6636 … +32.8398 | **no** | 0.057 |
| points | 20 | +15.2432 | −1.0066 … +32.3218 | **no** | 0.057 |
| % notional | 10 | +0.1809 | −0.0197 … +0.3925 | **no** | 0.073 |
| % notional | 20 | +0.1809 | −0.0234 … +0.3860 | **no** | 0.069 |
| R | 10 | **+0.0117** | −0.0198 … +0.0447 | **no** | 0.257 |
| R | 20 | +0.0117 | −0.0215 … +0.0442 | **no** | 0.278 |

**Non-overlapping subsample.** Thin the signal trades so no two holds overlap (≥10
sessions apart) and thin the population the same way; an iid bootstrap is then legitimate.
Two independent thinnings:

| thinning | n signal | n all | points margin | CI90 | R margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| offset 0 | 140 | 167 | **−12.2230** | −68.78 … +44.19 | −0.0368 |
| offset 5 | 138 | 166 | **−0.3438** | −56.49 … +54.30 | +0.0019 |

The sign of the margin **flips** when the overlap is removed, and the interval is an order
of magnitude wider than the point estimate. The effective independent signal sample is
about **56 trades**, not 554: 554 trades on a 10-session hold, entered on ~26% of
sessions, is roughly 56 non-overlapping episodes.

This is the central finding. The +15.24 pt margin is not distinguishable from zero under
any overlap-honest treatment, and in the risk-normalised unit that the paper book actually
judges (R), it is a 26% coin-flip.

## 2. Is the margin leveraged beta? — most of it is

Both arms take the identical trade: same 09:30 entry, same exactly-10-RTH-session hold,
same 4.0 × atr14 disaster cap, 1 micro. **Holding time and contract size are matched by
construction**, so the only exposure difference the filter can create is *how much index
moves through the position*: the volatility of the selected windows and the index level
at the time.

| | signal sessions | all sessions |
|---|---|---|
| atr14 | 133.27 | 127.04 |
| atr14 / entry price | **1.740%** | 1.645% |
| entry price | 7,405.85 | 7,470.13 |
| stop rate | **13.54%** | 10.94% |

The LOW-tercile filter selects sessions about **5.8% more volatile per unit of price** than
the average session — mechanically so: a close far below its own VWAP is more common on a
volatile session. It is buying more exposure per contract, and being stopped out more often
for it. Re-measuring at matched exposure:

- **notional-matched (% of entry):** +0.1809% — ≈ +13.5 pt at the sample's mean price.
  Notional matching costs little, because the filter picks slightly *cheaper* sessions.
- **volatility-matched (R = pts / 4·atr14):** **+0.0117 R** — ≈ **+5.9 pt** at the sample's
  mean risk of 508 pt.

So of the +15.24 pt headline margin, roughly **9.3 pt (61%) is the filter buying more
volatility**, and about 5.9 pt survives exposure matching. Chained onto the baseline: of the
+76.01 gross pt/trade the screen reported, ~80% is index drift, ~12% is volatility selection,
and ~8% is a residual whose 90% interval is −0.020 … +0.045 R. **The margin does not survive
conditioning on the same market exposure in any form that is distinguishable from noise.**

## 3. Subperiod stability — the points margin tracks the index level

margin = mean(signal) − mean(all sessions **in the same subperiod**), so each row has its own
contemporaneous baseline.

| year | n signal / all | points | % notional | R |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2015 | 76 / 212 | +8.378 | +0.2081 | +0.0196 |
| 2016 | 85 / 254 | **−0.303** | +0.0030 | **−0.0010** |
| 2017 | 63 / 253 | +19.621 | +0.3073 | +0.0950 |
| 2018 | 103 / 254 | +8.978 | +0.1418 | **−0.0091** |
| 2019 | 81 / 254 | +19.029 | +0.2514 | +0.0406 |
| 2020 | 88 / 255 | +39.737 | +0.4273 | +0.0412 |
| 2021 | 58 / 181 | +39.292 | +0.3261 | +0.0121 |
| first half (2015-02-02…2018-06-08) | 266 / 831 | +6.369 | +0.1175 | +0.0058 |
| second half (2018-06-11…2021-09-16) | 288 / 832 | +21.059 | +0.2238 | +0.0166 |

6 of 7 years are positive in points and 5 of 7 in R. That is *not* the clean one-regime
artifact the brief anticipated — but the points column rises with the index level (2020 and
2021 are 2.6× 2015 in points while being ordinary in R), which is exactly the artifact the
percent and R columns exist to strip out. In R the margin is negative in two of seven years
and **2017 alone is 48% of the sum of the seven annual R margins** (+0.0950 of +0.1984). A margin
carried by one year out of seven, in the only unit that is exposure-matched, is not a stable
edge; it is a wide distribution with a positive mean.

Stability is therefore **weak, not absent**: the sign is mostly positive, the magnitude is
not stable, and neither survives §1.

## 4. The project's own placebo suite

`src/integrity_checks.py:run_suite()` on the 554 signal trades in points, against the
**correct null** — the own-drift baseline mean 60.7698, not zero — with the full 1,663-session
population and the selector mask supplied so the placebo leg could actually run.

**No check in this suite reads VXN or the macro calendar, so none of the seven was gated by
the reference-data block.** (The gate blocks the s.7 Salvage menu's conditions 1 and 4, which
is a different instrument and is dealt with below.) Seven attempted: 4 green, 2 RED, 1 not
applicable.

| check | status | what it says |
|---|---|---|
| 1. drift_null | GREEN | sign survives the correct null (+76.013 → +15.243). This is the screen's own gate restated, not new evidence. |
| 2. overnight_intraday_split | **NOT APPLICABLE** | the observations carry no overnight/RTH decomposition. A 10-session hold is all of both; inventing a split would be worse than declining. **Not a pass.** |
| 3. cost_sensitivity | **RED** | at 2× assumed costs the CI90 on the effect is 53.09 … 98.62 against a null of 60.77 — it crosses. Costs barely move the mean (76.013 → 76.006 on a 508-pt risk); the red is the **width** of the interval around a 15-pt margin, which is the same finding as §1 arriving through another door. |
| 4. roll_date_contamination | GREEN | 2.2% of observations on roll/short sessions against a 3.0% calendar base rate; excluding them does not flip anything. Genuinely informative. |
| 5. subperiod_stability | GREEN | **read the internals, not the label.** Split at the median trade, the first half averages 37.93 pt and the second 114.10 against the fixed 60.77 null — i.e. the margin is **−22.8 pt in the first half and +53.3 in the second**. It greens only because both halves' CIs are credible in the same direction relative to a *fixed* null, which is contaminated by the index level rising. §3's period-matched version is the honest one. |
| 6. concentration | **RED** | the top 5% of trades (28 of 554) carry **240%** of the effect. Per the check's own docstring, a share above 100% is the stronger version of the defect: the net margin is a small residual left after large offsetting contributions. 28 trades decide S004. |
| 7. placebo | GREEN — **and uninformative** | the inverted leg reaches −7.6147 against a real +15.2433. That is not evidence: for a difference-of-means statistic the inverted leg is an **arithmetic identity**, −margin × n_signal/n_other = −15.2433 × 554/1109 = −7.6147 exactly, so it can never exceed 50% of the real effect when the signal is a minority of the population and can never red. It is one hundredth of a point from the threshold by construction. The shifted t+1 leg reaches +6.2257 — **41% of the real effect** — while re-selecting 31% of the same sessions; it does gate, and it passes by a margin narrower than §1's uncertainty. |

The suite finds nothing S004 can hide behind. Its one strong green (roll dates) is about a
defect S004 never had; both reds and the honest reading of check 5 say the same thing as the
bootstrap.

## Verdict

The margin **does not survive overlap correction** (CI90 straddles zero at every block
length and in every unit; the sign flips under non-overlapping thinning; ~56 effective
observations, not 554), **does not survive exposure matching** (61% of it is the filter
buying more volatility; what remains is +0.0117 R with P(≤0) = 0.26), and is **not stable in
the exposure-matched unit** (negative in 2 of 7 years, three quarters of the total in 2017,
28 trades carrying 240% of the effect).

**S004 is a KILL.** It never entered PAPER, so there is no paper record and none was
adjusted. It is killed on measurement of its own frozen screen, not on a retune.

This is the fourth time this lineage has produced a large, clean, positive number that is
the index going up. The screen's baseline gate worked — it stripped 80% of the headline —
but a point estimate on overlapping windows was never going to finish the job, and the
screening agent said so in the ledger row before this file existed.
