## Read
This is the first result in the project's history that (a) comes from
the open scope Jason authorized (an instrument other than NQ), (b) uses
the magnitude-not-direction design meant to dodge the CPI/NFP failure
mode, and (c) actually passed Discovery. It is also, on its face, close
to the least surprising possible finding: "a scheduled data release
causes a volatility spike in the commodity it's about" is closer to
confirming a textbook fact than discovering an edge. The Statistical
stage's near-kill disclosure sharpens this: roughly half the effect is
the print itself, which is exactly what an efficient, fast market should
look like.

## Decision: CONTINUE, narrowly
Continue to Monetization and the Integrity Gate, but be explicit that
the honest expectation is this becomes a KNOWN TRUE / volatility-
conditioning-input fact (the same product class as the three already-
validated facts), not a tradeable directional edge, and quite possibly
closes at Monetization if there's no realistic way to convert "this hour
is 2x as volatile" into anything a stop-width or position-sizing rule
doesn't already handle via ATR itself (ATR is already forward-looking
research/studies's own conditioning inputs use a TRAILING ATR, which by
construction does NOT know a release is coming today -- so there may be
a real, narrow monetization path: a same-day, event-anchored volatility
adjustment on TOP of the trailing ATR baseline, only on CL, only in that
hour). That is the one thing worth the next research dollar here.
