# H118 — statistical power at the locked review point (UPGRADE 5)

Run September 11th, 2026 (interactive, Jason present). Statistical Agent.
Code: `src/h118_power_check.py`. Test: `tests/test_h118_power_check.py`.

**The review point does not move.** 40 resolved trades AND 12 calendar
months, one look. This note only says how likely that look is to answer
the question.

## Input

Per-trade R lists were never persisted; per-trade SD is backed out of each
stage's published 90% bootstrap CI (half-width = 1.645 x sd / sqrt(n)):

| stage | n | mean R | 90% CI | per-trade sd |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | 554 | +0.62 | [0.45, 0.79] | 2.37R |
| Validation | 213 | +0.28 | [0.05, 0.52] | 2.09R |
| Holdout | 187 | +0.40 | [0.14, 0.65] | 2.14R |

Pooled per-trade sd: **2.27R**. A 10-day time-exit trade is noisy relative
to its edge -- the signal-to-noise per trade is about 0.18.

## Result — probability the 90% CI clears zero at n = 40

| true effect | P(CI > 0), normal | P(CI > 0), skewed Monte-Carlo | n needed for 80% power |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.62R (Discovery figure, never quote) | 53% | 57% | 83 |
| **0.40R (Holdout re-baseline)** | **30%** | **31%** | **199** |
| 0.28R (Validation figure) | 19% | 20% | 406 |
| 0.14R (Integrity Gate floor) | 10% | 10% | 1622 |

At H118's observed signal rate (~7 trades/month in Holdout), 12 months is
roughly 80 resolved trades, not 40 -- the "whichever comes later" clause
means the calendar leg binds. Power at n = 80 if the true effect is 0.40R
is **~47%**; at n = 60, ~40%; at n = 100, ~53%.

## Reading

- The outside estimate ("~40-50%") was optimistic. If H118 is exactly as
  good as Holdout said, the locked look has roughly a **one-in-three
  chance** of confirming it at 40 trades and about a **coin flip** at the
  ~80 trades a year is likely to produce.
- An "inconclusive" result at the review point is therefore the single
  most likely outcome, and it must be read as *inconclusive*, not as
  failure: the review point was never sized to detect a 0.40R effect.
- Nothing about this changes H118's status, spec, or review point. It
  changes what Jason should expect from the slow lane: H118 is a bet that
  may well not resolve in 12 months. The fast lane (market map,
  short-horizon scans) is where a decision-grade answer can arrive
  sooner, because short-horizon candidates accumulate trades faster.

## What would NOT be acceptable

Extending the review point to 200 trades now, because the number looks
better, is moving a pre-registered review point after learning something
about it. It stays. A future *new* forward-test spec for a *different*
candidate should be sized from this table before it is frozen -- that is
the Statistical Agent's new mandatory power check (UPGRADE 3).
